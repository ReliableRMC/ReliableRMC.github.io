About Reliable RMC

Manufacturers of ready mix concrete at Mandir Hasaud, Chhattisgarh.

Strong Foundations. Reliable Construction.

Reliable RMC manufactures ready mix concrete at its batching plant opposite Indian Gas Godown, Mandir Hasaud.

We supply home owners, contractors and developers with concrete that is batched by weight, delivered by transit mixer and backed by test results. Whether it is one slab or a whole project, the aim is the same: the right mix, at your site, when you planned to pour.

What we do

Ready Mixed Concrete (RMC)

RMC Transportation

RMC Pumping
