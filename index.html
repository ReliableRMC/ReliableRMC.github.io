    Why Reliable

    The Standard Builders Depend On

    The right mix, on time, with proof of what was delivered.

    Weighed, not guessed

    Cement, sand, aggregate, water and admixture are batched by weight at the plant, load after load.

    Tested before it leaves

    Slump is checked at the plant, and cubes are cast from your pour for 7-day and 28-day strength.

    There when you booked

    Loads are spaced to match your placing speed, and dispatch calls you before each mixer leaves.

    A ticket with every load

    Grade, quantity and batching time on paper, so you have a record of what went into your structure.

    Help with the pour

    Not sure about the grade, the pump or the sequence? Talk to us before you book.

    Mixes to IS codes

    Designed to IS 10262 and supplied to IS 4926, for structures built to IS 456.
