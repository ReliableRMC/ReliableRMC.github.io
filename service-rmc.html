Before You Book

What you get, and what we need from you.

Good to know

Questions

Good to Know

Which grade should I order?

Your structural drawing names the grade for each element. If you do not have one yet, call us and we will talk you through the usual choices.

How long can concrete wait at site?

Plan to place each load within about two hours of batching. The batching time is printed on the ticket that comes with it.
