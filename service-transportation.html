Questions

Good to Know

What access does a transit mixer need?

A firm approach road wide enough for a loaded mixer, no low cables, and a level place to stand while it discharges.

Can you deliver at night?

Night pours are arranged by prior booking, so the plant, mixers and pump are ready for you.
